# The School Website Handbook

A plain-English guide to running your school's website from the admin panel.

No technical knowledge assumed. If a word needs explaining, it is explained
where it first appears.

## 1. The five ideas you need

Everything in the admin panel is one of five things. Once these click, the
rest of the panel explains itself.

### 1.1 A **website**

One school, one website. The panel can hold several — a senior wing, a junior
wing, a second campus — and each has its own logo, colours, pages and content.
Nothing you type into one ever appears in another.

The name of the website you are currently editing is shown at the very top of
the screen, next to a small building icon. **If you have more than one website,
check this before you start typing.** It is the single most common way to edit
the wrong school by mistake.

### 1.2 A **design**

The design decides the *shape* of your home page — where the banner sits, how
big the headings are, whether the menu runs across the middle or down one side.
There are seven, and you can switch between them at any time without losing a
single word of your content.

Think of it as changing the frame around a painting. The painting stays.

### 1.3 A **section**

Your home page is built from 30 stacked blocks called sections — the banner,
the principal's message, the photo gallery, the news list, and so on.

Each one can be **switched on or off**, **reordered**, and **filled in**
separately. A section that is switched off, or that you have left empty, simply
does not appear — there is no gap and no placeholder. Visitors cannot tell it
exists.

You will not use all 30. Most schools use eight to twelve. That is normal and
correct.

### 1.4 A **content list**

Some things are not one-offs — news items, events, departments, staff members.
These live in their own lists under **Content** in the sidebar, and the home
page pulls from them automatically.

This matters: if you add a news article to the News list, it appears on the
home page **and** on the news page **and** in any menu link pointing there,
without you touching any of them.

### 1.5 A **setting**

Anything that applies to the whole website rather than to one block — your
colours, the scrolling notice bar, the popup that greets visitors, the hover
effects. These live in **Site settings**.

## 2. Signing in and finding your way

### Getting in

Go to your admin address (your IT contact will have given you this) and sign
in with your email and password. If you have forgotten the password, an
administrator can reset it from **User accounts**.

### The sidebar

The menu down the left is grouped into four parts. Here is what each item is
for, in one line each.

**School website** — the day-to-day work

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | The front page of the panel. Shortcuts and a summary. |
| **Branding** | Logo, colours, school name, address, phone, design. |
| **Home page** | The 30 sections of your home page. |
| **Pages** | About, Admission, Facilities, Contact and any others. |
| **Contact inbox** | Enquiries visitors have sent you. |
| **Navigation** | The menu bar across the top of the website. |

**Content** — lists that feed several places at once

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Media library** | Every picture and file you have uploaded. |
| **News & notices** | Circulars, results, announcements. |
| **Events** | Sports day, annual day, parent meetings. |
| **Departments** | Science, Commerce, Primary Wing, and so on. |
| **Faculty & staff** | Teachers and staff, with photographs. |

**Configuration** — set once, changed rarely

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Site settings** | Notice bar, popup, colours, effects, admissions. |
| **SEO settings** | Your title and description on Google. |

**Platform** — only visible to senior administrators

| Item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Websites** | Create a new school website. |
| **Website templates** | Ready-made starting points. |
| **Domains** | Which web address shows which website. |
| **Page gallery** | Which starter pages new websites get. |
| **User accounts** | Who can sign in, and what they may do. |
| **Tenants** | Separate organisations on the platform. |

### Saving

**Nothing is saved until you press the Save button** at the bottom of the
form. There is no autosave. If you navigate away mid-edit, your changes are
gone.

Changes appear on the live website **immediately** after you save. There is no
publishing step and no queue. Refresh the public site in another tab and it is
there.

> **Tip:** keep the public website open in a second browser tab while you work.
> The button at the top right of the panel opens it.

## 3. Branding

**Where:** School website → Branding
**What it changes:** things that appear on *every* page of the website.

| Field | Where it shows up | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Website title** | Browser tab, header, footer, search results | Your school's full name. |
| **Site key** | In the address, when one domain hosts several schools | Lower-case, no spaces. Leave alone unless told otherwise. |
| **Website type** | Wording in a few places (School vs College) | |
| **Home design** | The whole shape of the home page | See section 3.1 below. |
| **Tagline** | Small line under the school name in the header | Keep it short — three to five words. "We learn to serve". |
| **Logo URL** | The crest in the header | Use **Browse** to pick from your media library. |
| **Favicon URL** | The tiny icon on the browser tab | A small square image. |
| **Header / banner image URL** | The big picture behind the home page banner | Only used when the banner has no slideshow of its own. |
| **Primary color** | Headings, menu, footer background | Your main school colour. |
| **Secondary color** | Buttons, links, small accents | Your second colour. |
| **Footer text** | The line at the very bottom | Usually a copyright line. |
| **Address** | Footer and contact page | |
| **Phone** | Header, footer and contact page | Visitors tap it to call. |
| **Email** | Header, footer and contact page | Visitors tap it to write. |
| **Map embed URL** | The map on the contact page | See section 14.6 for how to get one. |
| **Social links JSON** | Footer and contact page | Prefer setting these in **Site settings** instead. |

### 3.1 The seven designs

Changing this changes the layout only. Every word, picture and section stays
exactly where it was. You can switch back immediately if you dislike it.

| Design | What it looks like | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| **Classic** | Wide photo banner, a strip of numbers, welcome, gallery | Most schools |
| **Modern** | Banner photo beside the headline, then course cards | Schools with strong photography |
| **Campus** | Tall banner, large facility panels underneath | Big campuses |
| **Academic** | Results-led banner, numbers and department columns | Colleges, senior schools |
| **Prestige** | Centred framed banner with a leadership quote | Heritage institutions |
| **Bulletin** | Dense and practical — notices beside the welcome, tight headings | Schools publishing circulars and timetables |
| **Atrium** | Spacious and editorial — big type, full-size principal's portrait, a wall of photographs | Schools whose site reads like a prospectus |

**Try this:** switch design, look at the public site, switch back if you don't
like it. It costs you nothing and takes ten seconds.

## 4. Home page

**Where:** School website → Home page

You will see all 30 sections listed in order. For each one you can:

- **Switch it on or off** — off means invisible to visitors, but your content
  is kept safe for when you switch it back on
- **Change its position** — move it up or down the page
- **Edit it** — open it and fill in the fields

### 4.1 What every section has in common

Open any section and you will find the same four panels:

**Content & messaging**
- **Section title** — the big heading visitors see
- **Subtitle** — a smaller line under it (optional)
- **Description** — a rich-text box for paragraphs, with bold, italics, lists
  and links

**Call to action**
- **Button text** — leave blank for no button
- **Button destination** — where the button goes. Either a page on your own
  site (`/admission`) or a full web address (`https://…`)

**Section configuration**
- The fields that belong to this section alone. Every section's are listed
  below.
- Some sections also have **repeating rows** — a list you add to, one row per
  course, per photograph, per staff member. Use **Add row** to add, and the
  cross to remove.
- **Advanced JSON configuration** is a technical escape hatch. **You should
  never need it.** Ignore it.

**Media assets**
- **Primary image** — the section's main picture. Drop a file or choose one.
- **Background image** — a photograph behind the whole section. A white veil
  is drawn over it automatically so your words stay readable.

### 4.2 Every section, one by one

Below, "fields" means what appears under **Section configuration**, and "rows"
means the repeating list.

---

#### 1. Hero Banner
**What it is:** the big picture at the very top of the home page — the first
thing anyone sees.

**Fields:** Hero heading · Supporting line · Primary button · Secondary button ·
Slide change (seconds) · Background video URL

**Rows:** Image · Image description

**How it works:** add one row per picture and it becomes a slideshow, changing
every few seconds. Add none and it uses the single Header image from Branding.
"Slide change" of `0` stops it moving.

**Important:** if your banners are finished posters that already have their own
words printed on them, turn on **"Banner pictures carry their own text"** in
Site settings. That removes the heading, the buttons and the shading, and shows
each poster whole and uncropped. Otherwise your heading is printed on top of
theirs.

---

#### 2. Welcome Section
**What it is:** a short paragraph welcoming visitors, usually with a photograph.

**Fields:** Eyebrow label (a small word above the heading, e.g. "Welcome") ·
Image description

---

#### 3. About School
**What it is:** a longer piece about the school's history and character.

**Fields:** Eyebrow label · Image description

---

#### 4–7. Principal / Chairman / Director / Manager Message
**What they are:** four identical sections for four different people. Use the
ones you need and leave the rest off.

**Fields (each):** Name · Designation · Featured quote

**How it works:** the **Featured quote** is the sentence displayed large in
quotation marks. The **Description** box holds the full message underneath.

#### 8. Statistics
**What it is:** the strip of big numbers — students, teachers, and so on.

**Fields:** Students · Teachers · Placements · Years of excellence

**How it works:** enter plain numbers (`1200`, not `1,200+`). They count up as
the visitor scrolls to them.

#### 9. Courses
**Rows:** Course title · Description · Page URL · Image URL

**How it works:** one row per course or stream. **Page URL** is optional — fill
it in and the card becomes clickable.

#### 10. Departments
**Rows:** Department · Description · Page URL · Image URL

**Note:** there is also a **Departments** list under Content. Use that one if
you want departments to appear in several places; use these rows for a quick

#### 11. Why Choose Us
**What it is:** the three or four reasons families should pick you.

**Fields:** Section introduction · Desktop columns (use 3 or 4)

**Rows:** Reason · Description · Icon name

#### 12. Announcements
**Rows:** Announcement · Date · Link · Summary

---

#### 13. Latest News
**Fields:** none of its own beyond the title and intro

**Rows:** News title · Summary · Date · Link · Image URL

> **⚠ Read this one carefully.** If you have anything in **Content → News &
> notices**, those articles are shown here and the rows above are **ignored**.
> The section only controls the heading and the intro line.
>
> To change the news items on your home page, go to **Content → News &
> notices**, not to this section. The rows here are a fallback for schools that
> do not use the news list at all.

#### 14. Upcoming Events
**Rows:** Event title · Date · Link · Location

> Same rule as news: if **Content → Events** has anything in it, those are
> shown and these rows are ignored.

---

#### 15. Gallery
**Rows:** Image title · Image URL · Image description

**How it works:** one row per photograph. Use the **Browse** button to pick
from your media library rather than pasting addresses — pictures hosted
elsewhere can disappear without warning.

**If you see caption text where a picture should be**, that picture's address
is broken. Replace it.

---

#### 16. Video Section
**What it is:** one or more films — a campus tour, an annual day recording.

**Fields:** Line under the heading · Video URL · Poster image URL · Caption

**Rows:** Caption · YouTube, Vimeo or file URL · Poster image URL

**How it works:**
- For **one video**, fill in the Video URL field and leave the rows empty.
- For **several**, add a row each. The single fields are then ignored.
- Paste the address straight from your browser's address bar. All of these
  work: `youtube.com/watch?v=…`, `youtu.be/…`, a YouTube Shorts link, a Vimeo
  link, or a video file you uploaded yourself.
- **Poster image** is the still picture shown before someone presses play.
  YouTube supplies its own, so you can leave it blank for YouTube videos.
- Nothing loads from YouTube until a visitor actually presses play. This keeps
  your page fast.
- If a link is not one this system recognises, that video is skipped rather
  than shown broken.

## 5. Pages

**Where:** School website → Pages
**What it is:** every page other than the home page.

Each page has:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The heading, and the name in the menu |
| **Slug** | The address. `about` gives you `yourschool.edu/about`. Lower-case, hyphens instead of spaces, no spaces or symbols. |
| **Page type** | Decides the layout. See below. |
| **Template key** | For technical use. Leave alone. |
| **Excerpt** | A one-line summary, used in listings |
| **Page content (HTML)** | The body of the page |
| **Menu order** | Lower numbers appear earlier in the menu |
| **SEO title** | The heading Google shows |
| **SEO description** | The grey paragraph Google shows underneath |

### Page types

| Type | What you get |
|---|---|
| **About** | A straightforward article layout |
| **Admission** | Article plus the admissions details from Site settings |
| **Facilities** | A grid of facilities |
| **Messages** | Layout for principal's / chairman's messages |
| **Gallery** | A photograph grid |
| **Disclosure** | Table layout for mandatory board disclosures |
| **Committee** | Layout for committee member lists |
| **Contact** | Contact details, a map, and an enquiry form |
| **Custom** | A blank page — you supply everything |

> **Changing the slug changes the page's address.** Anyone who bookmarked the
> old one, and any link you have printed, will stop working. Change it once,
> early, then leave it alone.

## 6. Content lists

These four lists feed the home page, their own pages, and menu links, all at
once. Add something here and it appears everywhere it belongs.

### 6.1 News & notices

Circulars, results, admission announcements.

Each item has a headline, a summary, a full body, a date and a category.

**Appears on:** the home page (Latest News section), the news page, and any
menu link pointing to news.

**Remember:** items here override anything typed into the Latest News home
section.

### 6.2 Events

Sports day, annual day, parent-teacher meetings.

Each has a title, date, location and description.

**Appears on:** the home page (Upcoming Events section) and the events page.
Same override rule as news.

### 6.3 Departments

Science, Commerce, Primary Wing, and so on. Each has a name, description and
picture.

### 6.4 Faculty & staff

Teachers and staff with photographs, designations and qualifications.

**Use this** when you want photographs and detail. **Use the Staff List home
section** when you just want a plain table of forty names.

## 7. Media library

**Where:** Content → Media library

Every picture and file you have uploaded, in one place.

**To upload:** press Upload, choose your file, wait for it to finish.

**To use a picture anywhere:** wherever you see a **Browse** button next to an
image field, press it and pick from here. This is always better than pasting a
web address, because:

- pictures hosted on someone else's site can vanish without warning
- your own uploads load faster and are always available

**Sensible sizes:**

| Where | Suggested size |
|---|---|
| Banner / hero | 1920 × 1080 pixels |
| Section pictures | 1600 × 900 |
| Gallery | 1200 × 800 |
| Portraits | 600 × 600 |
| Logo | Around 300 pixels tall, transparent background |

Keep files under about 500 KB where you can. Large pictures make pages slow,
especially on phones.

## 8. Contact inbox

**Where:** School website → Contact inbox

Everything visitors have sent through any form on the website — the contact
page and the popup form both land here.

Each entry shows the name, email, phone, subject and message, with the date.
Unread ones are marked, and the count appears at the top of the panel.

**These are not emailed to you automatically.** Check this page regularly, or
you will miss enquiries.

If you set up **Enquiry types** in Site settings, each message says which kind
it was — an admission enquiry, a job application, feedback — so you can sort
the inbox without reading every one.

## 9. Navigation

**Where:** School website → Navigation

The menu across the top of the website.

For each menu item:
- **Label** — the word visitors see
- **URL** — where it goes (`/about`, or a full `https://…` address)
- **Order** — lower numbers appear further left
- **Parent** — set this to make a drop-down under another item

**Keep it short.** Nine or ten items is the practical maximum before the menu
wraps onto a second line and starts to look untidy. Everything else belongs in
a drop-down or in the footer.

## 10. Site settings

**Where:** Configuration → Site settings

Everything that applies to the whole website. Grouped exactly as the page is.

### 10.1 Announcement bar

The coloured strip at the very top of every page.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Notice text** | What it says. Separate several notices with a vertical bar: `Admissions open \| Results out` |
| **Label** | A short word at the start, in the accent colour — "NOTICE", "LATEST". Blank for none. |
| **Scroll the text across the bar** | On makes it move; off keeps it still |
| **Copies on the strip** | One, two, three, or "fill the strip". A short notice reads best as one copy; a long one is better filling the strip, or a gap opens in the middle |
| **Seconds for one pass** | How slowly it crosses. Higher is slower. |
| **Notice bar colour** | Leave blank for your primary colour |
| **Notice bar style** | Solid, gradient, dark or outline |

### 10.2 Admissions

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Open, closed, or opening soon |
| **Enquiry phone / email** | Shown on admission pages |
| **Office hours** | Shown on the contact page and in the footer |
| **Prospectus / brochure** | The PDF families download |
| **Online application link** | Where "Apply now" buttons lead |
| **Enquiry types** | One per line. Becomes a drop-down on your forms, so you can tell an admission question from a job application. |

### 10.3 Social and messaging

Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp number.

**These appear in the footer and on the contact page**, as icons. Paste the
full address of your page. Leave blank for any you do not use.

### 10.4 Appearance and motion

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Logo height (pixels)** | Between 28 and 160. A bigger crest hangs below the header over the banner; the header does not get taller. |
| **Crest shape** | **Rounded** (a circle behind it), **Square** (a rounded panel), or **Original** (the artwork alone, nothing behind it). Choose Original if your crest already has its own outline. |
| **Button colour / style / shape / hover** | How every button looks and behaves |
| **Card hover / Picture hover / Link hover** | What happens when the mouse passes over |
| **Hover colour** | The colour used by those effects |
| **Default section entrance** | How sections appear as you scroll |
| **Default section background** | A pattern behind sections that have no picture of their own |
| **Fade sections in as the visitor scrolls** | Turn off if you prefer no movement |
| **Banner pictures carry their own text** | Shows each banner whole with nothing printed over it. For finished poster artwork. |
| **Phone and email in the header** | Shows them beside the logo as "Call us" / "Mail us". **Leave this on** — there is no other place at the top of the page for them. |
| **Header button** | Text and destination for the button in the header, e.g. "Admission Enquiry" |

### 10.5 Opening popup

The window that greets visitors when the page opens.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show the popup** | On or off |
| **Poster image(s)** | One address per line, or separated by a vertical bar. Several become a slideshow. |
| **Seconds on each poster** | How long before it changes |
| **Heading / link** | Optional. A link makes the poster clickable. |
| **Add an enquiry form beside the poster** | Puts a form next to the picture |
| **Form heading** | e.g. "Request a callback" |
| **Show once per visit** | On means a visitor sees it once, not on every page |
| **Close by itself after (seconds)** | Blank means it waits to be closed |

**How the poster is sized:** it is never cropped and never squashed. Shown on
its own it takes the full width of the popup; beside a form it takes half. If
your poster is tall and thin, the popup becomes tall and thin to match.

### 10.6 Hero slideshow

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Seconds on each picture** | Used when the Hero section has no setting of its own |
| **Show arrows and dots** | Lets visitors move through the pictures themselves |

## 11. SEO settings

**Where:** Configuration → SEO settings

How your school appears in Google results.

| Field | Advice |
|---|---|
| **Meta title** | Under "Default metadata". Your school name and city. Under 60 characters. |
| **Meta description** | One or two sentences about the school. Under 160 characters. This is the grey text under your link in Google. |
| **Keywords** | Largely ignored by search engines now — safe to leave blank. |
| **Canonical URL** | Your main web address. |
| **Open Graph image** | The picture shown when someone shares your link on WhatsApp or Facebook. 1200 × 630 pixels. Shown under the heading "Sharing image". |
| **Allow search engines to index this website** | Under the heading "Indexing". **Must be on** for Google to list you. Turn it off only while building a site you do not want found yet — and remember to turn it back on. |

## 12. Platform pages

Only senior administrators see these.

### 12.1 Websites

Create a new school website. You choose the name, the site key, the type, the
design and which starter pages it gets.

### 12.2 Website templates

Complete ready-made websites — colours, fonts, sections and sample content
already filled in. Pick one when creating a website and you start with a
finished-looking site instead of empty boxes.

### 12.3 Domains

Which web address shows which website.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Domain** | The address, e.g. `noida.cambridgeschool.edu.in`. Host name only — no `https://`, no path. |
| **Serves** | Which website it shows. Bind it to one and that school gets clean addresses like `/about`. Leave it unbound and one address serves every website under its own path, like `/school/about`. |
| **Primary** | The canonical address used in links and search results |
| **Status** | Live, or switched off without deleting |

**To change which website an address shows:** press **Edit**, change **Serves**,
press **Save domain**.

**To remove an address:** press **Remove** and confirm. Nothing is blocked —
you can remove a school's last address if you mean to. The page will warn you
above the list when a website has been left with no address at all.

### 12.4 Page gallery

Which starter pages a newly created website is given.

### 12.5 User accounts

Who can sign in and what they may do. Give each person their own account —
shared logins make it impossible to tell who changed what.

### 12.6 Tenants

Separate organisations on the platform. Most schools will never touch this.

## 13. Reverse lookup

**"I can see it on the website — where do I change it?"**

| What you see | Where to edit it |
|---|---|
| Scrolling coloured strip at the very top | Site settings → Announcement bar |
| Crest / logo | Branding → Logo URL. Size and shape: Site settings → Appearance |
| School name and tagline in the header | Branding → Website title, Tagline |
| "Call us" / "Mail us" in the header | Branding → Phone, Email. Turn on/off in Site settings |
| Button in the header | Site settings → Header button |
| The menu bar | Navigation |
| Big picture at the top of the home page | Home page → Hero Banner |
| Window that pops up when the site opens | Site settings → Opening popup |

## 14. Common jobs

### 14.1 Put a new notice on the site

1. **Content → News & notices → Add**
2. Headline, summary, full text, date, category
3. Save

It now appears on the home page, on the news page, and anywhere else news is
shown. You do not need to touch the home page at all.

### 14.2 Change the banner picture

**One picture:** Branding → Header / banner image URL → Browse → pick → Save.

**A slideshow:** Home page → Hero Banner → Section configuration → add a row
per picture → set "Slide change (seconds)" → Save.

**If your banners are finished posters with their own words on them:** also
turn on Site settings → "Banner pictures carry their own text". This shows each
one whole and stops the site printing a second heading over yours.

### 14.3 Add photographs to the gallery

1. **Content → Media library** → upload the pictures
2. **Home page → Gallery** → add a row per picture
3. **Browse** to pick each one, add a short description
4. Save

The description is read aloud to visually impaired visitors and appears if a
picture fails to load. It is worth writing.

### 14.4 Add a video

1. Open the video on YouTube and copy the address from the browser bar
2. **Home page → Video Section**
3. One video: paste into **Video URL**. Several: add a row each
4. Save

### 14.5 Add a new page

1. **School website → Pages → Add**
2. Title, slug (e.g. `transport`), page type, content
3. Save
4. **School website → Navigation** → add a menu item pointing at `/transport`

A page with no menu item exists but is very hard to find. Always do step 4.

### 14.6 Put your campus on the map

1. Go to Google Maps and find your school
2. **Share → Embed a map → Copy HTML**
3. From that HTML, copy only the address inside `src="…"`
4. Paste into Branding → Map embed URL

### 14.7 Change the school's colours

**Branding → Primary color / Secondary color.** Use hex codes like `#0a438d`.
Primary is your main colour; secondary is for buttons and accents.

Check the result on the live site. A very pale primary can make white text hard
to read.

### 14.8 Turn a section off

**Home page** → find the section → switch it off. Your content is kept. Turn it
back on whenever you like and everything is exactly as you left it.

### 14.9 Change the whole look of the home page

**Branding → Home design** → pick another → Save. Look at the site. Change it
back if you prefer. No content is lost either way.

## 15. Things that surprise people

**News and events override the home page sections.**
If Content → News has articles, the Latest News home section shows those and
ignores anything you typed into its rows. Same for Events. Edit the list, not
the section.

**Nothing is emailed to you.**
Enquiries go to the Contact inbox and stay there. Check it.

**Empty sections vanish completely.**
There is no gap, no placeholder, no "coming soon". If a section is not on the
site, it is either switched off or empty.

**Changes are live immediately.**
There is no draft mode and no preview-then-publish. Save means published.

**Pasted picture addresses can break.**
A picture hosted on someone else's website can be moved or removed without
telling you, and then your page shows caption text instead. Upload to the
Media library and use Browse.

**The slug is the address.**
Changing it breaks every existing link to that page — bookmarks, printed
prospectuses, links from other sites. Choose it once.

**Turning off "Allow search engines to index this website" hides you from Google.**
Useful while building. Disastrous if forgotten.

**The website you are editing is shown at the top.**
If your organisation has more than one school on the platform, check it before
typing.

## 16. When something looks wrong

| What you see | Almost always because | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Caption text where a picture should be | The picture's address is broken | Replace it, preferably from the Media library |
| A section is missing from the site | It is switched off, or it is empty | Home page → switch it on / fill it in |
| The menu wraps onto two lines | Too many items | Move some into drop-downs or the footer |
| My edit did not appear | Not saved, or you are looking at a cached page | Press Save; refresh with Ctrl-F5 (⌘-Shift-R on a Mac) |
| The home page changed shape unexpectedly | The design was changed | Branding → Home design → set it back |
| A heading is printed over my poster | The banner is being treated as a photograph | Site settings → turn on "Banner pictures carry their own text" |
| The banner looks washed out or dimmed | A background picture has been set on the Hero section, which draws a veil over it | Home page → Hero Banner → Media assets → clear the background image. The banner belongs in the Hero picture or Branding, not the section background. |
| No phone number at the top of the page | "Phone and email in the header" is off | Site settings → Appearance → turn it on |
| A visitor says the site is unreachable | The web address may have been unbound | Domains → check that address is Live and Serves the right website |

## Getting help

When reporting a problem, include:

1. **Which website** (the name at the top of the panel)
2. **Which page** — the exact address, e.g. `yourschool.edu/about`
3. **What you expected** and **what you got**
4. **A screenshot**, showing the whole browser window
5. **Which browser**, and phone or computer

That turns a half-hour of guessing into a five-minute fix.
